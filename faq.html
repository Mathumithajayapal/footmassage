The Basics

General & Booking

You can book an appointment easily through our online portal on the "Contact" page, by calling our studio directly at 1-800-RELAX-NOW, or by walking in. However, we highly recommend booking in advance to secure your preferred time.
We require a 24-hour notice for all cancellations or reschedules. Cancellations made within 24 hours of the appointment time will incur a fee equal to 50% of the scheduled service cost. No-shows will be charged the full amount.
Yes, we offer digital gift cards which can be purchased on our website and sent directly to the recipient via email, as well as beautifully packaged physical gift cards available for purchase at our front desk.
We ask that you arrive 10-15 minutes prior to your scheduled time. This allows you enough time to check in, use the restroom, enjoy a cup of herbal tea, and begin relaxing before your therapist brings you back.
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and cash. Please note that exact change is appreciated if paying in cash.
Walk-ins are always welcome! However, our availability fluctuates daily. If we do not have an immediate opening, our front desk will gladly schedule you for the next available slot or later in the week.
