Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the monthly membership billing work?

Your card will be charged on the same date every month. There are no long-term contracts, and you can pause or cancel your membership anytime with a 7-day notice before your next billing cycle.

Do my unused membership sessions roll over?

Yes! If you miss a month, your unused sessions roll over to the next month for up to 90 days, ensuring you never lose the value of your membership.

Is tipping included in the price?

Gratuity is not included in our standard pricing or membership packages. Tipping is entirely at your discretion, but always appreciated by our hardworking therapists.

What is your cancellation policy?

We require a 24-hour notice for any cancellations or rescheduling. Appointments canceled within 24 hours will incur a 50% cancellation fee.
