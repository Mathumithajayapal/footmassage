Quick Answers

Common Questions

Do I need to prepare before a session?

We recommend arriving 10 minutes early to relax. Avoid heavy meals right before your appointment.

Is reflexology painful?

Our therapists adjust to your comfort level. You may feel a "good hurt" on tense pressure points, but it should never be agonizing.

Can I mix treatments?

Yes! Many clients combine our deep foot reflexology with a 15-minute hot stone add-on for maximum relief.
